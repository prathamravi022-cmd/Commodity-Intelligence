## Modifications

Every file is a crop and a downscale of the original, re-encoded to WebP at quality 76. Nothing is recoloured, retouched or composited, and no photograph is presented as market data or as the specification of an MCX contract.

## Use in the interface

- Photographs are context for physical bullion only. Prices, spreads, z-scores and backtest results always come from the pipeline outputs in `data/`, never from an image.
- Contract cards caption their photograph as illustrative: contract size, quoted unit and purity come from `config/config.yaml`.
- Every photograph is rendered with the author and licence underneath it, and `app/artwork.py` is the only place they are declared.
